# Wildwood Blast — Privacy and Support

Effective: 2 November 2026

Wildwood Blast is published by the HappyDogGame developer account. Questions and privacy requests: **alexhappydog@gmail.com**. Include “Wildwood Blast” in the subject. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

## Your game on your device

No game account is required. Puzzle progress, scores, language and audio preferences, Finn’s growth, garden upgrades, wardrobe items, virtual currency and purchase-delivery records are saved on your device. We do not operate a game account server or cloud-save service for this version. Removing the app removes local progress; device backups may also contain app data.

## Advertising choices

Unity Ads provides optional rewarded videos and occasional ads between completed games or missions. Advertising does not initialize until you choose to enable ads. You can stop new ad requests in the game’s advertising privacy controls. Ads already opened may finish using data already transmitted. Removing interstitial ads is a separate one-time purchase; optional rewarded videos remain available.

The game requests ads without personalized-ad consent and does not request Apple tracking permission or deliberately access the advertising identifier. This does not mean advertising is data-free. When ads are enabled, Unity and its providers may process device or SDK identifiers, IP/network information and approximate country or region, device characteristics and language, ad views and interactions, app usage, performance and diagnostic information. Unity’s disclosures also describe purchase-related and other technical information depending on enabled SDK features. These data support ad delivery, measurement, service operation, security and fraud prevention. Ad reporting may collect information you choose to send.

Read Unity’s [Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) and [privacy FAQ](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-faq) for its data handling, retention, international processing and available choices or deletion requests.

## Apple purchases

Apple processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card or banking details. The app handles product and transaction information to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate delivery, and stores delivery records locally. Restore purchases restores eligible non-consumable ad removal; consumable stardust and ordinary game progress are not restored by that button. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Speech, permissions and support

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location access. Character speech uses Apple’s speech synthesis; the app does not record your voice. Support emails are used to respond and resolve your request. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. Third-party requests may need to go to Unity or Apple, whose data we cannot delete ourselves.

## Children and changes

This is a general-audience puzzle game, not a dedicated children’s service. Advertising and purchases should be supervised as appropriate. Apple provides device purchase restrictions. Contact support if you believe a child has provided personal information to us. This page will be updated when our practices change.

## Help

Drag a piece from the tray to the board. A full row or column clears regardless of ordinary colors and symbols. Adventure keeps its missions and companion systems. Classic refills after all three pieces are placed; Blast replaces each placed piece and adds countdown bombs. For an interrupted purchase, reopen the shop. Restore purchases applies to ad removal. For help, email the product name and a redacted receipt if needed.
